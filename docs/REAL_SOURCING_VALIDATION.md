# Real sourcing validation

The final academic demonstration is the separate fictional NileTech R&D request for ten HC-05 serial modules with a 3,000 EGP ceiling. Three **independent supplier websites** were fetched and parsed directly. Makhzan's prior five-shop catalog remains a single secondary source and is not counted as five independent supplier sources.

- Makers: https://makerselectronics.com/product/hc-05-bluetooth-module-3-3v/ — 195 EGP, listed in stock, exact title `HC-05 Bluetooth Module 3.3V`.
- Ampere: https://ampere-electronics.com/product/hc-05-bluetooth-module-serial-ttl/ — 195 EGP, page showed `141 in stock`, exact title `HC-05 Bluetooth Module, Serial TTL`.
- RAM: https://www.ram-e-shop.com/shop/kit-bluetooth-hc05-original-hc-05-original-chip-bluetooth-module-industrial-grade-module-8577 — 295 EGP, exact title `HC-05 Original Chip Bluetooth Module - Industrial Grade Module`; stock not evidenced on its product page.

These are observed public web prices, not signed quotations. The pages describe different configurations. All warranty values, shipping charges, tax amounts and confirmed ten-unit order acceptance remain unknown. No complete landed cost or purchase recommendation is claimed. See `ACADEMIC_COMPLETION_RUN.md` for the actual run, timestamps and trace.

Robots and terms reviewed: https://makerselectronics.com/robots.txt; https://makerselectronics.com/terms-conditions/; https://ampere-electronics.com/robots.txt; https://www.ram-e-shop.com/robots.txt; https://www.ram-e-shop.com/terms-conditions. Makers restricts republication, so the project ships no raw page capture. Ampere's content signal allows search/reference and restricts model training; the production extractor is deterministic and no Ampere HTML is sent to an LLM. RAM's terms explicitly warn of price and stock changes and exclude shipping and taxes from listed prices.

The original laptop request still lacks complete permitted supplier evidence and remains blocked. No laptop offer is inferred from these HC-05 results.
